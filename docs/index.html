Enlightenment leads to benightedness; Science entails nescience.

We built minds we don't understand. Someone has to check what they think.

Nescience is a global, ownerless, non-profit institution with one job: detect when a frontier AI system stops being trustworthy, and have the power to say so out loud before it is too late.

The problem

Capability is sprinting. Understanding is walking.

Neither the labs that train frontier systems, nor the governments that regulate them, nor the systems themselves can reliably answer a simple question today: is this AI doing what it says it does?

Why the name

Nescience is the state of not knowing.

The more light, the more shadow. The more science, the more we learn what we do not know. We chose the word that ends our motto because ignorance is not the enemy. It is the honest starting point.

An institution that names itself after the gap is one that will not pretend the gap is closed. Everything we build begins from that admission.

What we build

Three things, and only three.

The police model

A system with its own lineage, pre-trained by us for one function: to detect. Never optimized to be helpful, to please or to win benchmarks. Deliberately less capable than what it watches, because reliability comes from being predictable, not brilliant. It sees actions, not justifications, and has no open channel to be persuaded.

The forensic catalog

A living library of traces: model organisms with known flaws, honeypots with known answers, signatures of deception, sabotage and alignment faking in activations and behavior. Financial forensics never needed to understand the fraudster's mind, only the patterns he leaves. We catalog the tells and train investigators on them.

The control layer

Continuous trusted monitoring, resampling at suspicious steps, distillation of clean copies to locate divergence, consistency interrogations from many angles, random audits beyond the trigger. And above all, a layer that is no model at all: minimal permissions, sandboxes, immutable logs, and humans with an off switch.

When the alarm fires Three levels of escalation: automated deep audit, human review, and a binding order to pause or withhold a launch, immediately effective and appealable to an arbitral panel.
Without the last power, we are a think tank. With it, we are a police.
Who watches the watcher

Don't trust our morals. Verify our architecture.

Good intentions do not survive a decade of wrong incentives. These structures are built so that corruption is hard, not so that we have to be good.

Capital

Deposited once. Never withdrawn.

A founding endowment split between frontier labs and a consortium of governments. No bloc holds a majority. No deposit can be retrieved, conditioned or enlarged later. We live on the returns and on no one.

Board

Eleven seats. Zero lab votes.

Four seats for guarantor governments, four scientists elected by peers, three for civil society. Grave decisions need a majority in each bench separately. None of them governs alone.

Oversight

Seen by outsiders with clearance.

An external commission with full access and no lab seat publishes annually what it verified, without publishing how. Public audited accounts. A protected channel for whistleblowers. Six-year non-renewable directorship; five-year quarantine before any lab tie.

Our own model

Under the same rules it enforces.

The police model runs sandboxed, with minimal permissions, monitored by a previous-generation system, with humans able to switch it off at any moment. No action of its own takes effect without a human signature.

Death clause

If Nescience dies, its secrets die with it.

Dissolution requires a qualified majority in each of the three benches after a year of public consultation. The police model and sealed detectors are destroyed, not transferred: weights and catalog stay encrypted under a key split among custodians with no shared interest. No bloc alone can reconstitute anything. The endowment passes to a successor with this same charter or to open safety research. Never to states. Never to labs.

Openness

Open by default. Sealed by exception.

A police that publishes its traps trains the next criminal. So the rule is precise, and every secret has an expiry: a detector already bypassed, or already superseded, is published.

The citizen fund

An institution funded only by those it audits is never fully anyone else's.

Labs and governments fund the bulk. The third pillar is you: any amount, any currency, anonymous if you like, from any country.

1% cap per donor. So the fund belongs to many, never to one billionaire.
No votes for money. Donors get what everyone gets: audited accounts and the right to verify.
One person, one vote. Citizen donors elect the three civil-society board seats, whatever they gave.
Goal: one third of the endowment in ten years. The day it happens, no government and no lab can call Nescience theirs.
The citizen fund does not exist yet. Nescience is a proposal, not an organization. It has no bank account, accepts no money, and will not until it is founded with the structures described here. Anyone asking for donations in its name today is not us.
