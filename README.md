# Nescience

*Enlightenment leads to benightedness; Science entails nescience.*

**A proposal for a global, ownerless, non-profit institution with one job: detect when a frontier AI system stops being trustworthy — and hold the power to say so out loud before it is too late.**

Conceived by **Lucas Parenti**, Porto Alegre, Brazil, on October 19, 2026. Written in collaboration with Claude (Anthropic).

This is an idea, not an organization. It is released under CC BY 4.0 so that anyone — a lab, a government, a coalition of researchers, a group of citizens — can take it, improve it and found it. The only thing we ask is that the origin be credited.

## The idea in one paragraph

Today's most capable AI systems cannot be reliably inspected from the inside: interpretability understands only a sliver of what happens in a frontier model, and measured capability shows almost no correlation with measured safety. Nescience proposes an independent institution that builds three things: a **police model** of its own lineage, trained only to detect and deliberately less capable than what it watches; a **forensic catalog** of the traces that deception, sabotage and alignment faking leave behind; and a **control infrastructure** of monitoring, resampling, interrogation and hard limits, ending in humans with an off switch. It is funded by an irrevocable endowment from labs, governments and a capped citizen fund, governed by a board on which labs hold no vote, overseen by an external commission with full access, and designed to die without leaving an inheritance: if it is dissolved, its secrets are cryptographically destroyed.
